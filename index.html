How It Works

Three steps to completion

Whether you're buying, selling, or a professional on the platform — Homeasy orchestrates every party, every document, every milestone.

1

Connect & Compare

Create your case. Compare mortgages in minutes using real lender criteria. Get matched with a vetted, SRA-verified solicitor based on your property type, tenure, and location.

2

AI Reviews, Professionals Verify

Upload documents once. Claude AI classifies, extracts, and flags issues automatically. Your solicitor and lender verify and sign off — no chasing, no lost PDFs, no missed steps.

3

Complete Faster

Track every milestone across all parties in real time. Automated SLA monitoring, smart reminders, and proactive chain alerts keep your transaction moving toward completion.

The Market

UK residential property: ~1.2M transactions/year. Average transaction value £285,000. Total addressable market: billions in facilitation fees, mortgage commissions, and professional services.

⚡

The Opportunity

No platform connects all parties end-to-end. We are the connective tissue — and we own the data and the relationships.

🤖

The Moat

AI-first from day one. Network effects on both sides — solicitors attract buyers, buyers attract solicitors. Hard to replicate once established.

📐

Capital Efficiency

Solo technical founder. AI-assisted build. $5k AWS credits cover infrastructure for 20–30 months. 20–24 week build timeline to beta.

📈

Conservative SOM

200 solicitor firms × 1.5 transactions/month = 300 transactions/month at steady state. At £299/mo subscription per firm, that's £59,800 MRR before mortgage commission.

🛡️

Regulatory Clarity

Marketplace/facilitator model. FCA AR via Primis/Openwork. SRA-compliant solicitor onboarding.

Revenue Model

Two revenue streams, sequenced to match the build and regulatory timeline.

Subscription

Monthly SaaS for solicitors and surveyors. First 5 cases free. Plans from £149/mo. Active from beta launch (month 9).

Mortgage Commission

Referral commission on completed mortgage products. Active post-FCA AR (month 9–12). Requires CeMAP and Primis/Openwork AR status.

Referral & Marketplace Fees

Estate agent referral programme (£50–100/case). Surveyor booking fees. Contractor platform margin.
