Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Homeasy Ltd is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Who We Are

Homeasy Ltd is a company registered in England and Wales. We operate a property transaction platform at homeasy.co.uk. For data protection enquiries, contact us at admin@homeasy.co.uk.

2. Data We Collect

We may collect and process the following categories of personal data:

  • Identity data: name, date of birth, government-issued ID (for KYC/AML purposes)
  • Contact data: email address, phone number, postal address
  • Financial data: income information, mortgage application details, payment records
  • Transaction data: property details, case status, document uploads
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, usage data
  • Communications: emails, messages sent through the platform

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data to:

  • Provide and operate the Homeasy platform and facilitate property transactions
  • Verify your identity and comply with Anti-Money Laundering (AML) obligations
  • Connect you with solicitors, surveyors, lenders, and other professionals
  • Process payments and manage billing
  • Send transactional communications (case updates, reminders, alerts)
  • Improve our services and conduct analytics
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data on the following legal bases under UK GDPR:

  • Contract performance — processing necessary to provide our services
  • Legal obligation — AML, KYC, and regulatory compliance requirements
  • Legitimate interests — fraud prevention, platform security, service improvement
  • Consent — marketing communications (where applicable)

5. Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. AML and transaction records are retained for 6 years post-completion in accordance with the Money Laundering Regulations 2017. You may request erasure of your data subject to our legal retention obligations.

6. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored on AWS infrastructure in the eu-west-2 (London) region, ensuring UK data residency. We apply encryption at rest and in transit, access controls, and regular security reviews. We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

7. Third-Party Sharing

We share your data only where necessary with:

  • Solicitors, surveyors, and lenders involved in your transaction (with your consent)
  • Identity verification providers (Onfido or equivalent) for KYC purposes
  • Payment processors (Stripe) for billing and payouts
  • AWS for cloud infrastructure and document storage
  • Regulatory bodies where legally required

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

8. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to: access your data, correct inaccurate data, request erasure (subject to legal obligations), object to processing, data portability, and to withdraw consent. To exercise any of these rights, contact admin@homeasy.co.uk.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the platform evolves. Material changes will be communicated via email or a notice on the platform. Continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For any privacy-related queries: admin@homeasy.co.uk

Cookie Policy

Cookie Policy

Last updated: March 2026

This policy explains how Homeasy Ltd uses cookies and similar technologies on homeasy.co.uk. By using our website, you consent to the use of cookies in accordance with this policy.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognise your device and remember information about your visit. We also use similar technologies such as local storage and session storage.

2. Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the platform to function. They cannot be disabled. They include:

  • Session cookies for authentication and security
  • Load balancing cookies to distribute traffic across our servers
  • CSRF protection tokens to prevent cross-site request forgery

Analytics Cookies

We use analytics tools to understand how users interact with the platform. This helps us improve performance and usability. Analytics data is anonymised and not used for advertising. We use self-hosted analytics infrastructure where possible to keep data within the UK.

Functional Cookies

These cookies remember your preferences (such as language or display settings) to improve your experience on return visits.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some third-party services we integrate may set their own cookies, including:

  • Stripe — for payment processing
  • Identity verification providers — for KYC checks

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not share cookie data with advertising networks.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling strictly necessary cookies may prevent parts of the platform from functioning correctly. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies by adjusting settings; please refer to your browser's help documentation for instructions.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as our technology and legal obligations evolve. We will notify users of material changes via the platform or by email.

6. Contact

For cookie-related queries: admin@homeasy.co.uk

Compliance

Compliance

Last updated: March 2026

Homeasy Ltd is committed to operating in full compliance with applicable UK laws and regulations. This page summarises our key regulatory commitments.

Data Protection (GDPR)

Homeasy Ltd is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) as a data controller. We process personal data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. All data is stored on UK-based infrastructure (AWS eu-west-2, London). See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Anti-Money Laundering (AML)

Homeasy operates as a property marketplace and is subject to the Money Laundering, Terrorist Financing and Transfer of Funds (Information on the Payer) Regulations 2017. We conduct identity verification (KYC) on all users via regulated third-party providers and maintain records in accordance with our legal obligations. Suspicious activity is reported to the National Crime Agency (NCA) where required.

Financial Services (FCA)

Homeasy Ltd operates under an FCA Appointed Representative arrangement for regulated mortgage referral activities. Mortgage comparison information provided on the platform is for indicative purposes only and does not constitute financial advice. Homeasy does not hold its own FCA authorisation for advising on or arranging regulated mortgage contracts — this is provided through our principal firm relationship. For regulated mortgage advice, you should seek guidance from a qualified mortgage adviser.

Legal Services (SRA)

Homeasy is a technology platform and marketplace facilitator and is not a regulated law firm. Homeasy does not provide legal advice. All legal services on the platform are provided by independently regulated solicitor firms authorised and regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority (SRA). Homeasy does not supervise or take responsibility for legal advice given by solicitors on the platform.

Surveying (RICS)

Surveyors listed on the Homeasy platform are verified as members of the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors (RICS). Survey reports are prepared and owned by the relevant RICS-qualified professional. Homeasy does not endorse or guarantee the accuracy of survey reports.

Consumer Protection

We are committed to fair and transparent practices in accordance with the Consumer Rights Act 2015 and the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013. Our platform fees and professional service fees are clearly disclosed before any commitment is made.

Complaints

If you have a complaint about our platform or services, please contact us in the first instance at admin@homeasy.co.uk. We will acknowledge your complaint within 5 business days and aim to resolve it within 28 days. If your complaint relates to a regulated professional on the platform, you may also contact the relevant regulatory body (SRA, RICS, or FCA) directly.
